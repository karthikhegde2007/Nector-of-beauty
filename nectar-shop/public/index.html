A hidden special store

ಹುಡುಕಿ ಬನ್ನಿ, ಇಷ್ಟವಾಗುತ್ತದೆ

From the road it looks like any shop in Saraswati Towers. Step in on the ground floor and the store opens up: glass shelves lit edge to edge, clearly signed aisles and a glowing logo wall at the back.

Look for the pink Nectar of Beauty board above the Karnataka Bank ATM, between Om Dental Clinic and Hira Cut-Piece Centre.
